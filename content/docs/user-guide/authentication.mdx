---
title: Authentication
description: Create, verify, access, and recover a MamaBear customer account.
---

## Create an account

Open `/register`, enter your account information, and submit the form. Phone-number fields accept numeric input validated by the registration flow. After registration, follow the email verification instructions before signing in.

## Verify your email

The verification link opens `/auth/verify-email`. The page validates the token carried by the link and reports whether verification succeeded. If the link is invalid or expired, request a new verification flow rather than editing its URL.

## Sign in

Open `/login` and use the credentials created during registration. A successful login creates a MamaBear session and returns you to the application. Protected account, checkout, and administration pages redirect unauthenticated visitors to login.

## Recover a password

1. Open `/forgot-password` and submit the email address associated with the account.
2. Use the reset link sent by the service.
3. The link opens `/auth/reset-password`, where you can choose a new password.
4. Return to `/login` and sign in with the new password.

For security, MamaBear does not reveal access or refresh tokens in the interface.
