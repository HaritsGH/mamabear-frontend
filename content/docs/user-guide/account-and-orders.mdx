---
title: Account, Addresses, and Orders
description: Maintain customer information, saved delivery addresses, order history, and invoices.
---

Account pages require an authenticated customer session.

## Account information

Open `/account/info` to review the profile information associated with the current account. Authentication details remain managed by the sign-in and recovery flows.

## Delivery addresses

Open `/account/addresses` to see saved addresses.

- Use `/account/addresses/new` to add an address.
- Open `/account/addresses/[id]/edit` to update an existing address.
- Choose an address label that helps distinguish home, office, family, or another delivery location.

![Address book listing saved delivery addresses](/images/docs/account-addresses.png)

Accurate recipient, phone, and regional information helps the checkout flow return valid shipping choices.

## Orders

Open `/account/orders` for order history and `/account/orders/[id]` for items, pricing, status, and timeline details. Order formatting reflects the status returned by the backend.

![My Orders page with status filter tabs](/images/docs/account-orders.png)

When available for the order, use the invoice download action to generate a PDF copy in the browser.
