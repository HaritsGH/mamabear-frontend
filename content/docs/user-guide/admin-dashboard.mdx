The `/admin` area is restricted to signed-in accounts whose role is `ADMIN` or `SUPERADMIN`. Other users are redirected to the storefront.

## Dashboard

`/admin/dashboard` summarizes store metrics, sales trends, top-selling products, recent orders, and low-stock products.

![Admin dashboard with summary cards and sales analysis chart](/images/docs/admin-dashboard.png)

## Catalog and promotions

- **Products:** list, filter, create, edit, delete, and perform supported bulk actions.
- **Categories:** list, create, edit, configure imagery and SEO information, and remove categories.
- **Promotions:** list, create, and edit promotional codes and their discount configuration.

![Admin product list with search, category filter, and CSV export](/images/docs/admin-products.png)

## Operations

- **Orders:** filter the order list, open order details, inspect items and timeline information, and apply supported status updates.
- **Customers:** search and filter customers, open detailed profiles, inspect order history and statistics, and manage supported customer status changes.

![Admin order list with search, status, sort, and date filters](/images/docs/admin-orders.png)

![Admin customer list with order count, total spend, and status](/images/docs/admin-customers.png)

## Reporting and settings

The reports area presents business summaries and charts for the selected reporting period. Settings are organized into store, shipping, payment, tax, and AI model sections.

![Reports page with revenue summary and revenue trend chart](/images/docs/admin-reports.png)

![Settings page showing store, shipping, payment, tax, and AI model tabs](/images/docs/admin-settings.png)

### AI assistant models

The **Model AI** tab controls which models power the customer chat assistant. Enter model IDs in OpenRouter format (`provider/model-name`); the tab links to openrouter.ai/models for the available IDs.

- **Model Jawaban Chatbot** writes the answers customers see.
- **Model Guardrail** screens questions (for example medical or off-topic ones) before they are answered.

Select **Simpan Perubahan** to save.

![Model AI settings tab with chatbot and guardrail model fields](/images/docs/admin-settings-ai.png)

## Administrators

The users area lists administrator accounts and provides the supported administrator creation flow. Access to a page does not imply permission beyond the actions offered by that page and accepted by the backend.
